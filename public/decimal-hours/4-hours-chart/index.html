4 hours and minutes as decimals

Four hours is a half-day shift, the length of many part-time and school-hours jobs. To convert 4 hours and any number of minutes, add the minutes ÷ 60 to 4. The table below does it in 5-minute steps and shows the pay at $15, $20 and $25 an hour.

Worked example: 4:25

4 hours 25 minutes

25 minutes ÷ 60 = 0.4167, so 4:25 = 4 + 0.4167 = 4.4167 hours (rounded to two places: 4.42).

Pay at $18.00 an hour: 4.42 × $18 = $79.56. Five days of 4:25 = 22.08 decimal hours (22:05) = $397.44.

Going the other way

To turn a decimal like 4.42 back into hours and minutes, keep the whole hours (4) and multiply the decimal part by 60: 0.42 × 60 = 25.2 minutes. Use the decimal hours converter for any value.

Frequently asked questions

What is 4:25 in decimal hours?

4.42 hours: 4 + 25/60.

What is 4 hours 30 minutes in decimal?

4.5 hours.

What is 4 hours 15 minutes in decimal?

4.25 hours.

What is 4 hours 45 minutes in decimal?

4.75 hours.

Why does my time card show 4.50 instead of 4:50?

Because the decimal .50 is half an hour (30 minutes). 4:50 equals 4.83 decimal hours.

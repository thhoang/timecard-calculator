10 hours and minutes as decimals

Ten hours is the shift length of a four-day, 40-hour week. To convert 10 hours and any number of minutes, add the minutes ÷ 60 to 10. The table below does it in 5-minute steps and shows the pay at $15, $20 and $25 an hour.

Worked example: 10:55

10 hours 55 minutes

55 minutes ÷ 60 = 0.9167, so 10:55 = 10 + 0.9167 = 10.9167 hours (rounded to two places: 10.92).

Pay at $18.00 an hour: 10.92 × $18 = $196.56. Five days of 10:55 = 54.58 decimal hours (54:35) = $982.44.

Going the other way

To turn a decimal like 10.92 back into hours and minutes, keep the whole hours (10) and multiply the decimal part by 60: 0.92 × 60 = 55.2 minutes. Use the decimal hours converter for any value.

Frequently asked questions

What is 10:55 in decimal hours?

10.92 hours: 10 + 55/60.

What is 10 hours 30 minutes in decimal?

10.5 hours.

What is 10 hours 15 minutes in decimal?

10.25 hours.

What is 10 hours 45 minutes in decimal?

10.75 hours.

Why does my time card show 10.50 instead of 10:50?

Because the decimal .50 is half an hour (30 minutes). 10:50 equals 10.83 decimal hours.

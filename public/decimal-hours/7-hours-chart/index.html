7 hours and minutes as decimals

Seven hours is a common paid day in jobs with a 7.5-hour schedule or a one-hour unpaid lunch on a shorter shift. To convert 7 hours and any number of minutes, add the minutes ÷ 60 to 7. The table below does it in 5-minute steps and shows the pay at $15, $20 and $25 an hour.

Worked example: 7:40

7 hours 40 minutes

40 minutes ÷ 60 = 0.6667, so 7:40 = 7 + 0.6667 = 7.6667 hours (rounded to two places: 7.67).

Pay at $18.00 an hour: 7.67 × $18 = $138.06. Five days of 7:40 = 38.33 decimal hours (38:20) = $689.94.

Going the other way

To turn a decimal like 7.67 back into hours and minutes, keep the whole hours (7) and multiply the decimal part by 60: 0.67 × 60 = 40.2 minutes. Use the decimal hours converter for any value.

Frequently asked questions

What is 7:40 in decimal hours?

7.67 hours: 7 + 40/60.

What is 7 hours 30 minutes in decimal?

7.5 hours.

What is 7 hours 15 minutes in decimal?

7.25 hours.

What is 7 hours 45 minutes in decimal?

7.75 hours.

Why does my time card show 7.50 instead of 7:50?

Because the decimal .50 is half an hour (30 minutes). 7:50 equals 7.83 decimal hours.

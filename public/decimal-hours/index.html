Hours and Minutes to Decimal Charts

Updated October 22, 2026 · Index · How we write

Choose the number of hours, then read the decimal for any minute.

Payroll multiplies decimal hours by a rate, but time cards show hours and minutes. Each chart below lists every minute for a given number of hours with the decimal value, plus pay at several rates. For minutes only (0-59) use the minutes to decimal chart.

The one-line formula

decimal = hours + minutes ÷ 60

15 minutes = 0.25, 30 = 0.50, 45 = 0.75. All other minutes are repeating or rounded decimals.

Frequently asked questions

What is 7:30 in decimal?

7.5 hours.

How do I convert minutes to decimal?

Divide by 60.

What is 15 minutes in decimal hours?

0.25 hours.

6 hours and minutes as decimals

Six hours is a short full day of work after an unpaid break is deducted. To convert 6 hours and any number of minutes, add the minutes ÷ 60 to 6. The table below does it in 5-minute steps and shows the pay at $15, $20 and $25 an hour.

Worked example: 6:35

6 hours 35 minutes

35 minutes ÷ 60 = 0.5833, so 6:35 = 6 + 0.5833 = 6.5833 hours (rounded to two places: 6.58).

Pay at $18.00 an hour: 6.58 × $18 = $118.44. Five days of 6:35 = 32.92 decimal hours (32:55) = $592.56.

Going the other way

To turn a decimal like 6.58 back into hours and minutes, keep the whole hours (6) and multiply the decimal part by 60: 0.58 × 60 = 34.8 minutes. Use the decimal hours converter for any value.

Frequently asked questions

What is 6:35 in decimal hours?

6.58 hours: 6 + 35/60.

What is 6 hours 30 minutes in decimal?

6.5 hours.

What is 6 hours 15 minutes in decimal?

6.25 hours.

What is 6 hours 45 minutes in decimal?

6.75 hours.

Why does my time card show 6.50 instead of 6:50?

Because the decimal .50 is half an hour (30 minutes). 6:50 equals 6.83 decimal hours.

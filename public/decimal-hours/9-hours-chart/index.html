Worked example: 9:50

9 hours 50 minutes

50 minutes ÷ 60 = 0.8333, so 9:50 = 9 + 0.8333 = 9.8333 hours (rounded to two places: 9.83).

Pay at $18.00 an hour: 9.83 × $18 = $176.94. Five days of 9:50 = 49.17 decimal hours (49:10) = $885.06.

Going the other way

To turn a decimal like 9.83 back into hours and minutes, keep the whole hours (9) and multiply the decimal part by 60: 0.83 × 60 = 49.8 minutes. Use the decimal hours converter for any value.

Frequently asked questions

What is 9:50 in decimal hours?

9.83 hours: 9 + 50/60.

What is 9 hours 30 minutes in decimal?

9.5 hours.

What is 9 hours 15 minutes in decimal?

9.25 hours.

What is 9 hours 45 minutes in decimal?

9.75 hours.

Why does my time card show 9.50 instead of 9:50?

Because the decimal .50 is half an hour (30 minutes). 9:50 equals 9.83 decimal hours.

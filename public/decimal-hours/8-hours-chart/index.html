8 hours and minutes as decimals

Eight hours is the standard paid workday, the figure behind the 40-hour week. To convert 8 hours and any number of minutes, add the minutes ÷ 60 to 8. The table below does it in 5-minute steps and shows the pay at $15, $20 and $25 an hour.

Worked example: 8:45

8 hours 45 minutes

45 minutes ÷ 60 = 0.75, so 8:45 = 8 + 0.75 = 8.75 hours (rounded to two places: 8.75).

Pay at $18.00 an hour: 8.75 × $18 = $157.50. Five days of 8:45 = 43.75 decimal hours (43:45) = $787.50.

Going the other way

To turn a decimal like 8.75 back into hours and minutes, keep the whole hours (8) and multiply the decimal part by 60: 0.75 × 60 = 45 minutes. Use the decimal hours converter for any value.

Frequently asked questions

What is 8:45 in decimal hours?

8.75 hours: 8 + 45/60.

What is 8 hours 30 minutes in decimal?

8.5 hours.

What is 8 hours 15 minutes in decimal?

8.25 hours.

What is 8 hours 45 minutes in decimal?

8.75 hours.

Why does my time card show 8.50 instead of 8:50?

Because the decimal .50 is half an hour (30 minutes). 8:50 equals 8.83 decimal hours.

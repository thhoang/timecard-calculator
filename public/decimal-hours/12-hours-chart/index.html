12 hours and minutes as decimals

Twelve hours is a long-rotation shift, and the point where double time can begin in California. To convert 12 hours and any number of minutes, add the minutes ÷ 60 to 12. The table below does it in 5-minute steps and shows the pay at $15, $20 and $25 an hour.

Worked example: 12:12

12 hours 12 minutes

12 minutes ÷ 60 = 0.2, so 12:12 = 12 + 0.2 = 12.2 hours (rounded to two places: 12.20).

Pay at $18.00 an hour: 12.2 × $18 = $219.60. Five days of 12:12 = 61 decimal hours (61:00) = $1,098.00.

Going the other way

To turn a decimal like 12.2 back into hours and minutes, keep the whole hours (12) and multiply the decimal part by 60: 0.20 × 60 = 12 minutes. Use the decimal hours converter for any value.

Frequently asked questions

What is 12:12 in decimal hours?

12.2 hours: 12 + 12/60.

What is 12 hours 30 minutes in decimal?

12.5 hours.

What is 12 hours 15 minutes in decimal?

12.25 hours.

What is 12 hours 45 minutes in decimal?

12.75 hours.

Why does my time card show 12.50 instead of 12:50?

Because the decimal .50 is half an hour (30 minutes). 12:50 equals 12.83 decimal hours.

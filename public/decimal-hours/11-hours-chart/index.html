11 hours and minutes as decimals

Eleven hours is a long shift where daily overtime may apply in some states. To convert 11 hours and any number of minutes, add the minutes ÷ 60 to 11. The table below does it in 5-minute steps and shows the pay at $15, $20 and $25 an hour.

Worked example: 11:05

11 hours 5 minutes

5 minutes ÷ 60 = 0.0833, so 11:05 = 11 + 0.0833 = 11.0833 hours (rounded to two places: 11.08).

Pay at $18.00 an hour: 11.08 × $18 = $199.44. Five days of 11:05 = 55.42 decimal hours (55:25) = $997.56.

Going the other way

To turn a decimal like 11.08 back into hours and minutes, keep the whole hours (11) and multiply the decimal part by 60: 0.08 × 60 = 4.8 minutes. Use the decimal hours converter for any value.

Frequently asked questions

What is 11:05 in decimal hours?

11.08 hours: 11 + 5/60.

What is 11 hours 30 minutes in decimal?

11.5 hours.

What is 11 hours 15 minutes in decimal?

11.25 hours.

What is 11 hours 45 minutes in decimal?

11.75 hours.

Why does my time card show 11.50 instead of 11:50?

Because the decimal .50 is half an hour (30 minutes). 11:50 equals 11.83 decimal hours.

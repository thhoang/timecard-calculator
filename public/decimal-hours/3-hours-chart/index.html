3 hours and minutes as decimals

Three hours often shows up as a short shift or a block of overtime on the end of a day. To convert 3 hours and any number of minutes, add the minutes ÷ 60 to 3. The table below does it in 5-minute steps and shows the pay at $15, $20 and $25 an hour.

Worked example: 3:20

3 hours 20 minutes

20 minutes ÷ 60 = 0.3333, so 3:20 = 3 + 0.3333 = 3.3333 hours (rounded to two places: 3.33).

Pay at $18.00 an hour: 3.33 × $18 = $59.94. Five days of 3:20 = 16.67 decimal hours (16:40) = $300.06.

Going the other way

To turn a decimal like 3.33 back into hours and minutes, keep the whole hours (3) and multiply the decimal part by 60: 0.33 × 60 = 19.8 minutes. Use the decimal hours converter for any value.

Frequently asked questions

What is 3:20 in decimal hours?

3.33 hours: 3 + 20/60.

What is 3 hours 30 minutes in decimal?

3.5 hours.

What is 3 hours 15 minutes in decimal?

3.25 hours.

What is 3 hours 45 minutes in decimal?

3.75 hours.

Why does my time card show 3.50 instead of 3:50?

Because the decimal .50 is half an hour (30 minutes). 3:50 equals 3.83 decimal hours.

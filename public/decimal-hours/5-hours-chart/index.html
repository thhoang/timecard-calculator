5 hours and minutes as decimals

Five hours is a long part-time shift and the point where many break rules start to matter. To convert 5 hours and any number of minutes, add the minutes ÷ 60 to 5. The table below does it in 5-minute steps and shows the pay at $15, $20 and $25 an hour.

Worked example: 5:30

5 hours 30 minutes

30 minutes ÷ 60 = 0.5, so 5:30 = 5 + 0.5 = 5.5 hours (rounded to two places: 5.50).

Pay at $18.00 an hour: 5.5 × $18 = $99.00. Five days of 5:30 = 27.5 decimal hours (27:30) = $495.00.

Going the other way

To turn a decimal like 5.5 back into hours and minutes, keep the whole hours (5) and multiply the decimal part by 60: 0.50 × 60 = 30 minutes. Use the decimal hours converter for any value.

Frequently asked questions

What is 5:30 in decimal hours?

5.5 hours: 5 + 30/60.

What is 5 hours 30 minutes in decimal?

5.5 hours.

What is 5 hours 15 minutes in decimal?

5.25 hours.

What is 5 hours 45 minutes in decimal?

5.75 hours.

Why does my time card show 5.50 instead of 5:50?

Because the decimal .50 is half an hour (30 minutes). 5:50 equals 5.83 decimal hours.

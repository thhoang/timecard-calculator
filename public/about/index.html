About

Time Card Calculator is a small, free tool site for people who track work hours: shift workers, freelancers, and small business owners who need a quick, honest time card.

The tools run entirely in your browser. There are no accounts, nothing to install, and your timesheet data never leaves your device.

What we build

  • A weekly time card calculator with breaks, overtime rules and pay estimates.
  • An hours-between-times calculator for quick answers.
  • A printable weekly timesheet you can save as a PDF.
  • An hourly-to-salary converter.

The site is supported by advertising, which keeps the tools free.

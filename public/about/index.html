About Time Card Calculator

Updated October 21, 2026 · Guide · How we write

A small, independent set of free tools for people who track work hours.

What this site is

Time Card Calculator is a collection of calculators and plain-English guides for shift workers, hourly employees, freelancers and small-business owners who need to total hours, check overtime or understand a timesheet. The tools run entirely in your browser, with no account, no sign-up and no data upload.

Many hour calculators give one number. We show the working, because the working is what lets you check a pay stub, catch a mistake or learn the method.

What we do not do

  • We do not give legal, tax or payroll advice. See the disclaimer.
  • We do not state a rule unless we can link to an official source. See the editorial policy.
  • We do not collect or sell your timesheet data. See the privacy policy.

What's here

Browse the calculators, the guides, and the quick-answer pages for shift hours, hours per year, hourly-to-annual pay and decimal hour charts.

The site is intended to be supported by advertising so the tools can stay free.

Corrections

If you find a mistake, tell us through the contact page. We correct errors and note material changes in the page's “last checked” date.

How to calculate yearly hours

hours per year = hours per week × weeks worked per year

Full-time at 40 hours is 2,080 hours; subtract unpaid weeks from 52 first if you have them. Hourly pay times yearly hours is your gross annual pay; the hourly to salary calculator does it for you.

Frequently asked questions

How many hours in a year at 40 hours a week?

2,080 hours: 40 × 52.

How many working hours are in a month?

About 173.3 for a 40-hour week: 2,080 ÷ 12.

Do unpaid weeks change the total?

Yes. Subtract the unpaid weeks from 52 before multiplying.

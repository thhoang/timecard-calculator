25 Hours a Week: Hours Per Year, Month and Pay Period

Updated October 22, 2026 · Quick-answer page · How we write

How many hours 25 a week adds up to over a month and a year, what it pays at common hourly rates and when overtime starts.

The answer

1,300 hours a year
25 hours × 52 weeks = 1,300. That is 108.3 hours a month on average and 50 hours every two weeks.

Twenty-five hours a week is five five-hour days, a typical school-hours schedule. This schedule is 62.5% of a 40-hour week and counts as part-time work.

Spread over a 5-day week that is 5 hours a day; over a 4-day week 6.25 hours a day; over 3 days 8.33 hours a day.

Worked example

25 hours a week at $20 an hour

Weekly gross = 25 h × $20 = $500.00. Annual = $500.00 × 52 = $26,000.00. Two unpaid weeks would remove $1,000.00, leaving $25,000.00.

Overtime

At 25 hours a week there is no federal overtime. You would need 15 more hours in a workweek to reach 40; any hours beyond that are overtime for non-exempt employees. See overtime rules and the calculator for state differences.

Frequently asked questions

How many hours a year is 25 hours a week?

25 hours a week × 52 weeks = 1300 hours a year, before subtracting any unpaid time off.

How many hours a month is 25 hours a week?

About 108.3 hours: 25 × 52 ÷ 12.

What is 25 hours a week per day?

5 hours a day over five days, or 6.25 hours over four days.

Is 25 hours a week full time?

There is no single federal definition in the sources we cite; employers define it for benefits. A 40-hour week is the standard comparison and 25 hours is 62.5% of that.

Do I get overtime at 25 hours a week?

Under the federal rule, non-exempt employees receive overtime for hours over 40 in a workweek. At 25 hours there is none.

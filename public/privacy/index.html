Privacy Policy

Updated October 22, 2026 · Guide · How we write

Last updated October 22, 2026.

Your timesheet and calculator data

Everything you type into a calculator is processed in your browser. It is not sent to our servers. The time card and timesheet remember what you entered by saving it in your browser's local storage on your own device, so it is still there when you return. Use the Reset button, or clear your browser's site data, to delete it. We cannot see or recover it.

Accounts and personal information

There are no accounts and no sign-up. We do not ask for your name, email or payment details. If you type a name or company into the printable timesheet, it stays on your device and appears only on the page you print.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

At the time of this update the site sets no cookies of its own, runs no analytics and shows no advertising. The site is intended to be supported by advertising in the future. If we add third-party advertising or analytics (for example Google AdSense), those services may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads, and we will update this policy before they are enabled and describe the choices available to you. Google explains how to manage personalised ads at adssettings.google.com.

Hosting logs

The site is served as static files by a hosting provider, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP address, requested page and time) for security and operations. We do not use those logs to identify visitors.

Children

The site is a general-audience tool and is not directed to children under 13.

Questions about this policy? Use the contact page.

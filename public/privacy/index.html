Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-20

Time Card Calculator is designed to collect as little as possible.

Your timesheet data

Times you enter are stored only in your own browser's local storage so the form keeps your entries between visits. This data is never sent to our servers - the calculators run entirely on your device. Clearing your browser data deletes it.

Cookies and advertising

We plan to show ads through third-party networks such as Google AdSense. If enabled, those networks may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on your visits. You can control personalized advertising in your Google ad settings.

Analytics

If we add privacy-friendly analytics, it will count page visits in aggregate. We do not build individual profiles.

Questions about this policy? See the contact page.

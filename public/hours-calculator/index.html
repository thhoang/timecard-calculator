How this was calculated

    How the hours calculator works

    hours = end time − start time − unpaid break

    Everything is converted to minutes after midnight, subtracted, and converted back. When you leave the dates blank and the end time is not after the start time, the calculator assumes the shift ends the next day (an overnight shift). When you fill in the start and end dates, it uses them exactly, so a span of several days works: from 6:00 pm Friday to 6:00 am Monday is 60 hours.

    Pick a break length and the tool subtracts it from the clock-to-clock time. Two extra fields scale the answer: days per week shows a weekly total, and an hourly rate shows pay for the shift.

    Worked examples

    1. A day shift

    8:00 am to 5:00 pm with a 60-minute lunch. Clock to clock: 9:00. Minus 60 minutes = 8:00 (8.00 hours).

    2. An overnight shift

    10:00 pm to 6:30 am with a 30-minute break. 10:00 pm to 6:30 am is 8:30. Minus 30 minutes = 8:00. No dates needed: the end time is earlier than the start, so the calculator adds the next day.

    3. Crossing several days

    Start Friday 6:00 pm, end Monday 6:00 am. Friday 6 pm → Saturday 6 pm is 24 h, → Sunday 6 pm is 48 h, → Monday 6 am is 60 h. Total 60:00 (60.00 hours), useful for on-call weekends.

    Hours between two times: the manual method

    1. Write both times in 24-hour form (5:00 pm is 17:00).
    2. Subtract the minutes first. If the end minutes are smaller than the start minutes, borrow one hour (60 minutes).
    3. Subtract the hours.
    4. If the result is negative, the end time is on the next day: add 24 hours.
    5. Subtract unpaid breaks.

    Example: 7:45 am to 4:20 pm. In 24-hour form 7:45 and 16:20. Minutes: 20 − 45 is negative, so borrow: 80 − 45 = 35 and the hour becomes 15. Hours: 15 − 7 = 8. Answer 8 hours 35 minutes (8.58 decimal hours).

    Daylight saving time

    Dates are treated as plain calendar days. If a shift spans the night the clocks change, the real elapsed time is one hour shorter or longer than the clock-based result. If you are paid for actual hours worked, adjust by one hour or ask payroll how that night is handled.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do you calculate hours between two times?

    Subtract the start from the end. If the end is earlier than the start the shift passes midnight, so add 24 hours. Then subtract unpaid breaks. The calculator shows each step.

    Is 8am to 5pm 8 or 9 hours?

    It is 9 hours clock to clock. With an unpaid one-hour lunch the paid time is 8 hours, and with a 30-minute lunch it is 8.5 hours.

    How do I calculate hours across midnight?

    Enter the real start and end times. If the end time is earlier than the start time, the calculator treats the end as the next day. 10 pm to 6 am is 8 hours.

    Can it calculate more than 24 hours?

    Yes: enter the start and end dates. The span can be as long as you need.

    What is the decimal hours result for?

    Payroll systems usually multiply decimal hours by the hourly rate. 7 hours 45 minutes is 7.75 decimal hours. Use the decimal hours converter for conversions without a start and end time.

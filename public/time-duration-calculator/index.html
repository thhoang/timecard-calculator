    How this was calculated

      Why hours and minutes are hard to add by hand

      Decimal arithmetic assumes ten of something makes the next unit, but minutes roll over at 60. Adding 1:45 and 2:30 as if they were decimals gives 3.75, but the correct answer is 4:15. The reliable manual method is to convert everything to minutes, add or subtract, then convert back.

      total minutes = hours × 60 + minutes    |    hours = total minutes ÷ 60 (whole part), minutes = remainder

      Worked examples

      Adding task times

      Start with 3:45, add 2:30, add 1:15. In minutes: 225 + 150 + 75 = 450 minutes = 7 hours 30 minutes = 7:30 (7.50 decimal hours).

      Subtracting a break

      A 9:15 shift minus a 0:45 unpaid break: 555 − 45 = 510 minutes = 8:30.

      What time will it be?

      10:15 pm plus 7 hours 45 minutes: 22:15 + 7:45 = 30:00. Subtract 24 hours: 6:00, which is 6:00 am the next day.

      Going backwards

      What time did a 6.5-hour job that finished at 2:10 pm start? 14:10 minus 6:30 = 7:40 am.

      Hours and minutes in everyday tasks

      Adding durations comes up more often than people expect: totalling a week of project tasks, adding travel time to a meeting, working out how long a flight and layover take, subtracting a lunch from a shift or checking how much overtime you did across several days. The principle is the same in each case: turn every duration into minutes, add or subtract, and turn the result back into hours and minutes.

      Example, travel plan: a 2 hour 50 minute drive, a 45-minute stop, and a 1 hour 35 minute ferry add to 170 + 45 + 95 = 310 minutes = 5 hours 10 minutes. If you leave at 7:20 am, the second calculator on this page shows an arrival at 12:30 pm.

      Time zones, days and long durations

      This calculator treats a day as exactly 24 hours (1,440 minutes). It does not know about time zones or daylight saving changes; if a task spans a clock change, adjust by an hour. Durations over 24 hours are shown both in total hours and as days plus hours, so 52:30 appears as 52:30 and 2d 4:30.

      Frequently asked questions

      How do I add hours and minutes?

      Convert to minutes, add, and convert back: 2:45 + 1:30 is 165 + 90 = 255 minutes = 4:15. Or let the calculator do it.

      How do I subtract time?

      Convert to minutes and subtract. 5:10 minus 1:40 is 310 − 100 = 210 minutes = 3:30.

      What is 1.5 hours in hours and minutes?

      1 hour 30 minutes. The decimal .5 is half of 60 minutes.

      Can the result be negative?

      Yes. If you subtract more than you started with, the answer shows a minus sign, for example -0:30.

      Does it work for time past 24 hours?

      Yes. Totals over 24 hours are shown in hours and also as days plus hours.

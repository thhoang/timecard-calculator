    How to print or save the timesheet as a PDF

    1. Type your name, company and the first day of the week.
    2. Enter clock-in and clock-out times and unpaid breaks for each day you worked.
    3. Press Print this result.
    4. In the print dialog choose Save as PDF as the destination (Chrome, Edge) or PDF → Save as PDF (Safari, macOS) to create a file you can e-mail. Choose your printer to print on paper.

    The printout contains the filled table, your totals, the calculation steps and two signature lines. Menus, buttons and advertising are hidden so it stays a one-page document.

    What a good timesheet contains

    The federal recordkeeping regulation for covered employees (29 CFR 516.2) requires employers to keep, among other items, the time of day and day of week on which the employee's workweek begins, the hours worked on each workday and the total hours worked each workweek. This timesheet records the date, time in, time out, unpaid break and daily total, and it prints the week-start date so the workweek is clear. Pay rate and pay amounts are usually kept by your employer's payroll system.

    For a walkthrough of each column and how to double-check the arithmetic by hand, read how to read a timesheet, or learn how to fill in a paper time card if your workplace still uses cards.

    Example of a completed week

    Part-time retail schedule

    Monday 9:00 am to 2:00 pm (no break) = 5:00; Wednesday 12:00 pm to 8:30 pm with a 30-minute break = 8:00; Saturday 10:00 am to 4:00 pm with a 30-minute break = 5:30. Total = 5:00 + 8:00 + 5:30 = 18:30 (18.50 hours). At $16.00/hour that is $296.00 gross for the week.

    Using the timesheet for different jobs

    • Employees: keep your own copy to check your pay stub against.
    • Freelancers and contractors: a signed timesheet attached to an invoice shows exactly what you billed. See tracking hours for freelancers.
    • Small business owners: collect one per employee each week; your payroll provider or accountant tells you what records to keep.

    A printable template for real-world situations

    Split shiftsEnter the full span (first clock-in to last clock-out) and put the gap in the break box. A 7:00 am to 11:00 am shift plus 3:00 pm to 6:00 pm is 11 hours clock to clock; enter a break of 240 minutes to get 7:00.
    Overnight workEnter the real times. 10:00 pm to 6:30 am is counted as 8 hours 30 minutes on the day it started.
    Unpaid lunchEnter the minutes; the printed total excludes them.
    Days offLeave the row blank. It prints as an empty line.

    Tip: if you need two weeks on one sheet, use the bi-weekly time card, which prints 14 dated rows.

    Keeping signed copies

    Print two copies if you want to keep one: one for the employer, one for yourself. Photograph signed pages and store them somewhere you can find later. If your employer has a required form, use this printout as your private cross-check and compare totals every period.

    Using the timesheet as a weekly habit

    People who check their hours weekly find mistakes while they are still easy to fix. Fill the timesheet on Friday afternoon (or at the end of your workweek), compare it with the hours you expect on your next stub and keep a copy. If the stub differs, you already have your own record. The same routine works for contractors who invoice weekly.

    For managers: reviewing a stack of timesheets

    Check that each sheet has the week start date, daily totals that match the in and out times, the break deducted and a signature. Look first at the weeks with the longest hours: that is where overtime and errors cluster. Compare the totals with the schedule, and ask the employee about any difference before payroll runs.

    Frequently asked questions

    Can I save this timesheet as a PDF?

    Yes. Press Print this result, then choose Save as PDF as the printer in the print dialog.

    Will my employer accept this timesheet?

    It has the standard fields: name, week, daily in/out, breaks, totals and signature lines. Many small employers accept a printed timesheet like this, but confirm with your employer or payroll provider.

    Is the data stored anywhere?

    Only in your own browser's local storage. Press Reset to remove it.

    Does the timesheet include overtime?

    Yes. Choose the overtime rule for your state; the totals show regular and overtime hours.

    Can I print more than one week?

    Fill one week, print it, then press Reset and start the next week. For two weeks on one card use the bi-weekly time card.

All Calculators

Updated October 22, 2026 · Index · How we write

Eighteen free tools for tracking, totalling and checking work hours.

Every calculator runs in your browser, shows its steps, links to a worked example and an explanation of the rules, and can be printed. Pick the one that matches your question.

Which calculator should I use?

  • I want my week or pay period totalled: time card calculator (or the bi-weekly and semi-monthly versions).
  • I want to know how long one shift is: hours calculator.
  • I think I am owed overtime: overtime calculator.
  • My payroll wants decimals: decimal hours converter.
  • I want to compare job offers: hourly to salary, salary to hourly and the paycheck estimator.

Frequently asked questions

Are the calculators free?

Yes, and there is no sign-up.

Do you save what I type?

Time cards are kept in your browser only. Nothing is sent to us.

Can I print a result?

Yes. Every calculator has a Print this result button; choose Save as PDF in the print dialog to keep a copy.

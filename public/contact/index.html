Contact

Updated October 22, 2026 · Guide · How we write

Report a bug, suggest a tool or flag a correction.

How to reach us

The easiest way is to open an issue on the project's public repository: https://github.com/thhoang/timecard-calculator/issues. Please include the page address, what you entered, what you expected and what you got.

Please do not post personal pay records or employer details: the calculators run locally, so we never see your numbers and you do not need to share them to report a problem.

What we can help with

  • A calculator that gives a wrong or confusing result
  • A rule on a guide that looks out of date, with a link to the official source
  • A tool you would like to see

We cannot give individual legal, tax or payroll advice. For wage-and-hour questions about your own job, contact your state labor department or the U.S. Department of Labor's Wage and Hour Division.

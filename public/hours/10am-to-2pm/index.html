10am to 2pm Is How Many Hours?

Updated October 22, 2026 · Quick-answer page · How we write

Clock-to-clock hours, paid hours after lunch, weekly and bi-weekly totals, pay at several hourly rates and overtime scenarios for a 10am to 2pm shift.

The answer for 10am to 2pm

4 hours
10am to 2pm (10:00 to 14:00) is 4 hours clock to clock. With a 30-minute unpaid lunch that is 3.5 hours; with a one-hour lunch, 3 hours.

A 10 am start is a late-morning shift, typical for retail, restaurants, salons and anywhere customers do not arrive until later in the day. Finishing at 2 pm leaves the whole afternoon free, which is why early shifts that end here suit second jobs, studying or school pick-ups. Four hours is a half shift. Shifts this short usually do not include an unpaid meal, so the clock-to-clock figure and the paid figure are normally the same.

Five paid days add up to 20 hours, which is 20 hours short of 40, so there is no weekly overtime under the federal rule. A sixth day brings the week to 24 hours, still within 40 hours.

Worked example

10am to 2pm at $23.50 an hour

A worker clocks in at 10am and out at 2pm. Clock to clock that is 4 hours (4:00). No unpaid meal is assumed on a shift this short.

Daily pay: 4 paid hours × $23.50 = $94.00.

Weekly (5 shifts): 20 hours = $470.00.

Bi-weekly (10 shifts): two workweeks of 20 hours each = $940.00 gross, before taxes.

Frequently asked questions

Is 10am to 2pm 4 hours?

Yes. 10am to 2pm (10:00 to 14:00) is 4 hours clock to clock. With a 30-minute unpaid lunch the paid time is 3.5 hours, and with a one-hour lunch it is 3 hours.

How many hours a week is 10am to 2pm, Monday to Friday?

Five days is 20 hours clock to clock, or 17.5 paid hours with a 30-minute unpaid lunch. Two weeks (ten shifts) is 40 hours clock to clock.

How much do you earn working 10am to 2pm at $20 an hour?

Assuming no unpaid meal, one shift pays $80.00 gross and a five-day week pays $400.00 before taxes.

Does 10am to 2pm lead to overtime?

Not on a five-day week: 20 paid hours is under 40. You would need about 10 shifts of this length in one workweek to reach 40 hours, and any hours beyond that are overtime under the federal rule.

Is the lunch break included in 10am to 2pm?

Usually it is not: an unpaid meal in which you are completely relieved of duty comes out of the span. Short paid rest breaks stay in. See the guide on calculating hours worked with breaks.

6am to 3pm Is How Many Hours?

Updated October 22, 2026 · Quick-answer page · How we write

Clock-to-clock hours, paid hours after lunch, weekly and bi-weekly totals, pay at several hourly rates and overtime scenarios for a 6am to 3pm shift.

The answer for 6am to 3pm

9 hours
6am to 3pm (06:00 to 15:00) is 9 hours clock to clock. With a 30-minute unpaid lunch that is 8.5 hours; with a one-hour lunch, 8 hours.

A 6 am start is the early shift: it is common in places that open before the rest of the town, such as bakeries, warehouses, construction sites and hospital morning handovers. A 3 pm finish is a half-way point between a short and a full day and lines up with the end of the school day. Nine hours clock to clock is the standard full day: with a one-hour unpaid lunch it is exactly eight paid hours, and with a 30-minute lunch it is eight and a half.

Worked example

6am to 3pm at $19.00 an hour

A worker clocks in at 6am and out at 3pm. Clock to clock that is 9 hours (9:00). A 30-minute unpaid meal brings paid time to 8.5 hours.

Daily pay: 8.5 paid hours × $19.00 = $161.50.

Weekly (5 shifts): 42.5 hours (40 regular + 2.5 overtime at $28.50) = $831.25.

Bi-weekly (10 shifts): two workweeks of 42.5 hours each = $1,662.50 gross, before taxes.

Frequently asked questions

Is 6am to 3pm 9 hours?

Yes. 6am to 3pm (06:00 to 15:00) is 9 hours clock to clock. With a 30-minute unpaid lunch the paid time is 8.5 hours, and with a one-hour lunch it is 8 hours.

How many hours a week is 6am to 3pm, Monday to Friday?

Five days is 45 hours clock to clock, or 42.5 paid hours with a 30-minute unpaid lunch. Two weeks (ten shifts) is 90 hours clock to clock.

How much do you earn working 6am to 3pm at $20 an hour?

Assuming a 30-minute unpaid meal, one shift pays $170.00 gross and a five-day week pays $875.00 before taxes, including $25.00 of overtime premium.

Does 6am to 3pm lead to overtime?

Working this shift five days a week gives 42.5 paid hours, which is over 40, so the extra 2.5 hours are overtime under the federal weekly rule. Some states also count hours over a daily limit; see the overtime calculator.

Is the lunch break included in 6am to 3pm?

Usually it is not: an unpaid meal in which you are completely relieved of duty comes out of the span. Short paid rest breaks stay in. See the guide on calculating hours worked with breaks.

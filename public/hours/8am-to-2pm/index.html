8am to 2pm Is How Many Hours?

Updated October 21, 2026 · Quick-answer page · How we write

Clock-to-clock hours, paid hours after lunch, weekly and bi-weekly totals, pay at several hourly rates and overtime scenarios for a 8am to 2pm shift.

The answer for 8am to 2pm

6 hours
8am to 2pm (08:00 to 14:00) is 6 hours clock to clock. With a 30-minute unpaid lunch that is 5.5 hours; with a one-hour lunch, 5 hours.

An 8 am start is the most familiar clock-in time: offices, shops, factories and public services all use it, so schedules built around it are easy to coordinate. Finishing at 2 pm leaves the whole afternoon free, which is why early shifts that end here suit second jobs, studying or school pick-ups. Six hours is a short shift; whether a meal is unpaid or paid depends on the employer and the state, so check your own schedule.

Five paid days add up to 27.5 hours, which is 12.5 hours short of 40, so there is no weekly overtime under the federal rule. A sixth day brings the week to 33 hours, still within 40 hours.

Worked example

8am to 2pm at $21.00 an hour

A worker clocks in at 8am and out at 2pm. Clock to clock that is 6 hours (6:00). A 30-minute unpaid meal brings paid time to 5.5 hours.

Daily pay: 5.5 paid hours × $21.00 = $115.50.

Weekly (5 shifts): 27.5 hours = $577.50.

Bi-weekly (10 shifts): two workweeks of 27.5 hours each = $1,155.00 gross, before taxes.

Frequently asked questions

Is 8am to 2pm 6 hours?

Yes. 8am to 2pm (08:00 to 14:00) is 6 hours clock to clock. With a 30-minute unpaid lunch the paid time is 5.5 hours, and with a one-hour lunch it is 5 hours.

How many hours a week is 8am to 2pm, Monday to Friday?

Five days is 30 hours clock to clock, or 27.5 paid hours with a 30-minute unpaid lunch. Two weeks (ten shifts) is 60 hours clock to clock.

How much do you earn working 8am to 2pm at $20 an hour?

Assuming a 30-minute unpaid meal, one shift pays $110.00 gross and a five-day week pays $550.00 before taxes.

Does 8am to 2pm lead to overtime?

Not on a five-day week: 27.5 paid hours is under 40. You would need about 7.3 shifts of this length in one workweek to reach 40 hours, and any hours beyond that are overtime under the federal rule.

Is the lunch break included in 8am to 2pm?

Usually it is not: an unpaid meal in which you are completely relieved of duty comes out of the span. Short paid rest breaks stay in. See the guide on calculating hours worked with breaks.

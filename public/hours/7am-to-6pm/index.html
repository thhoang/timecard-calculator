7am to 6pm Is How Many Hours?

Updated October 22, 2026 · Quick-answer page · How we write

Clock-to-clock hours, paid hours after lunch, weekly and bi-weekly totals, pay at several hourly rates and overtime scenarios for a 7am to 6pm shift.

The answer for 7am to 6pm

11 hours
7am to 6pm (07:00 to 18:00) is 11 hours clock to clock. With a 30-minute unpaid lunch that is 10.5 hours; with a one-hour lunch, 10 hours.

A 7 am start fits workplaces that want the day running by mid-morning, such as schools, clinics, trades and distribution centres, and it gets you ahead of the commute. A 6 pm finish extends coverage into the evening, useful for customer-facing teams and anyone who needs overlap with another time zone. Eleven hours is a long shift; five days of it is well over 40 hours even after an unpaid meal, so weekly overtime is likely.

Worked example

7am to 6pm at $21.75 an hour

A worker clocks in at 7am and out at 6pm. Clock to clock that is 11 hours (11:00). A 30-minute unpaid meal brings paid time to 10.5 hours.

Daily pay: 10.5 paid hours × $21.75 = $228.38.

Weekly (5 shifts): 52.5 hours (40 regular + 12.5 overtime at $32.62) = $1,277.81.

Bi-weekly (10 shifts): two workweeks of 52.5 hours each = $2,555.62 gross, before taxes.

Frequently asked questions

Is 7am to 6pm 11 hours?

Yes. 7am to 6pm (07:00 to 18:00) is 11 hours clock to clock. With a 30-minute unpaid lunch the paid time is 10.5 hours, and with a one-hour lunch it is 10 hours.

How many hours a week is 7am to 6pm, Monday to Friday?

Five days is 55 hours clock to clock, or 52.5 paid hours with a 30-minute unpaid lunch. Two weeks (ten shifts) is 110 hours clock to clock.

How much do you earn working 7am to 6pm at $20 an hour?

Assuming a 30-minute unpaid meal, one shift pays $210.00 gross and a five-day week pays $1,175.00 before taxes, including $125.00 of overtime premium.

Does 7am to 6pm lead to overtime?

Working this shift five days a week gives 52.5 paid hours, which is over 40, so the extra 12.5 hours are overtime under the federal weekly rule. Some states also count hours over a daily limit; see the overtime calculator.

Is the lunch break included in 7am to 6pm?

Usually it is not: an unpaid meal in which you are completely relieved of duty comes out of the span. Short paid rest breaks stay in. See the guide on calculating hours worked with breaks.

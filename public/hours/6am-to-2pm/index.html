Common questions

Is 6am to 2pm 8 hours?

Yes, 6am to 2pm is 8 hours clock to clock. Your paid working time is less if you take an unpaid break - with a 30-minute lunch it is 7.5 hours.

How many hours a week is 6am to 2pm, Monday to Friday?

Five days of 6am to 2pm is 40 hours clock to clock, or 37.5 paid hours with a 30-minute unpaid lunch each day.

Is lunch included in 6am to 2pm?

Usually not. In most US workplaces an unpaid meal break (commonly 30 or 60 minutes) comes out of the span, so 6am to 2pm with a one-hour lunch is 7 paid hours. Paid rest breaks do count as work time.

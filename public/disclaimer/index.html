Not legal, tax or payroll advice

The calculators, guides and examples on this site are for general information and estimation. They are not legal advice, not tax advice and not payroll advice, and they do not create any professional relationship. Wage-and-hour, tax and payroll rules depend on your job, your employer, your state and city, any union or contract, and the facts of your situation.

Estimates, not payslips

Results, including overtime splits and take-home pay, are estimates. Your employer's payroll system, your Form W-4, your state's rules and other deductions determine what you are actually paid. Always compare with your pay stub and confirm with your employer, payroll provider, state labor department or a qualified professional.

Rules change

We link to official sources and show the date we last checked them, but laws, regulations, tax tables and agency guidance change. Use the linked sources to confirm the current rule before relying on it.

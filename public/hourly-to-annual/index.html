Quick rule of thumb

Double the hourly rate and add three zeros for a fast annual estimate: $20 an hour → about $40,000 (exactly $41,600). For a precise result including unpaid weeks, use the hourly to salary calculator.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert hourly to annual?

Multiply by 2,080 for a full-time year (40 hours × 52 weeks).

What is $20 an hour per year?

$41,600 full time.

Are these gross or net amounts?

Gross, before taxes. Use the paycheck estimator for an estimate of take-home pay.

$25 an Hour Is How Much a Year?

Updated October 22, 2026 · Quick-answer page · How we write

Annual, monthly, bi-weekly and weekly pay at $25 an hour, with part-time schedules, overtime and an estimated take-home figure.

The answer

$52,000 a year
$25 an hour × 40 hours × 52 weeks = $52,000 gross, full time, before taxes. That is $4,333.33 a month, $2,000.00 every two weeks, and $1,000.00 a week.

Worked example: what a real schedule pays

$25 an hour at 38 hours a week

38 hours × $25 = $950.00 a week. Over 52 weeks that is $49,400, or $4,116.67 a month on average. With two unpaid weeks off, it falls to $47,500.

Overtime at $25 an hour

For a non-exempt employee under the federal weekly rule, hours over 40 are paid at 1.5 times the rate, or $37.50 an hour. A 45-hour week pays $1,187.50 (40 × $25 + 5 × $37.50), which is $62.50 more than straight time. A 50-hour week pays $1,375.00. Annualised over 52 weeks, a regular 45-hour week at $25 an hour is $61,750. State daily-overtime rules can add more; use the overtime calculator.

Estimated take-home pay at $25 an hour

Our paycheck estimator (federal tax only, single filer, no deductions, IRS 2026 Publication 15-T percentage method) estimates a bi-weekly check of about $1,690.85 from $2,000.00 gross: roughly $156.15 federal income tax withholding, $124.00 Social Security and $29.00 Medicare. State and local taxes, a different W-4 and benefit deductions will change the result. This is an estimate, not a payslip.

Putting $25 in context

  • $25 is 3.45 times the federal minimum wage of $7.25 per hour (U.S. Department of Labor). Your state or city minimum may be higher.
  • A $1 raise from $25 adds $2,080 a year at full time, a 4% increase.
  • You earn $0.417 per minute and need about 40 hours of work to gross $1,000.

Frequently asked questions

How much is $25 an hour a year?

$25 × 40 hours × 52 weeks = $52,000 a year gross, full time.

How much is $25 an hour a month?

About $4,333.33 a month before taxes: $52,000 ÷ 12.

How much is $25 an hour bi-weekly?

$2,000.00 per check: $25 × 80 hours.

How much is $25 an hour after taxes?

A federal-only estimate for a single filer is about $1,690.85 per bi-weekly check (see the paycheck estimator); your real figure depends on your state, W-4 and deductions.

What is $25 an hour with overtime?

Overtime hours are paid at $37.50 an hour for non-exempt employees under the federal weekly rule. A 45-hour week pays $1,187.50.

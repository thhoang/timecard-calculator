$40 an Hour Is How Much a Year?

Updated October 21, 2026 · Quick-answer page · How we write

Annual, monthly, bi-weekly and weekly pay at $40 an hour, with part-time schedules, overtime and an estimated take-home figure.

The answer

$83,200 a year
$40 an hour × 40 hours × 52 weeks = $83,200 gross, full time, before taxes. That is $6,933.33 a month, $3,200.00 every two weeks, and $1,600.00 a week.

Worked example: what a real schedule pays

$40 an hour at 22 hours a week

22 hours × $40 = $880.00 a week. Over 52 weeks that is $45,760, or $3,813.33 a month on average. With two unpaid weeks off, it falls to $44,000.

Overtime at $40 an hour

For a non-exempt employee under the federal weekly rule, hours over 40 are paid at 1.5 times the rate, or $60.00 an hour. A 45-hour week pays $1,900.00 (40 × $40 + 5 × $60.00), which is $100.00 more than straight time. A 50-hour week pays $2,200.00. Annualised over 52 weeks, a regular 45-hour week at $40 an hour is $98,800. State daily-overtime rules can add more; use the overtime calculator.

Estimated take-home pay at $40 an hour

Our paycheck estimator (federal tax only, single filer, no deductions, IRS 2026 Publication 15-T percentage method) estimates a bi-weekly check of about $2,590.82 from $3,200.00 gross: roughly $364.38 federal income tax withholding, $198.40 Social Security and $46.40 Medicare. State and local taxes, a different W-4 and benefit deductions will change the result. This is an estimate, not a payslip.

Putting $40 in context

  • $40 is 5.52 times the federal minimum wage of $7.25 per hour (U.S. Department of Labor). Your state or city minimum may be higher.
  • A $1 raise from $40 adds $2,080 a year at full time, a 2.5% increase.
  • You earn $0.667 per minute and need about 25 hours of work to gross $1,000.

Frequently asked questions

How much is $40 an hour a year?

$40 × 40 hours × 52 weeks = $83,200 a year gross, full time.

How much is $40 an hour a month?

About $6,933.33 a month before taxes: $83,200 ÷ 12.

How much is $40 an hour bi-weekly?

$3,200.00 per check: $40 × 80 hours.

How much is $40 an hour after taxes?

A federal-only estimate for a single filer is about $2,590.82 per bi-weekly check (see the paycheck estimator); your real figure depends on your state, W-4 and deductions.

What is $40 an hour with overtime?

Overtime hours are paid at $60.00 an hour for non-exempt employees under the federal weekly rule. A 45-hour week pays $1,900.00.
